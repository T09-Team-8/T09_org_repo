# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the KindDesign project and community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other people.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback graciously.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to people affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, and project-related community activities. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report an incident privately to **kinddesign.cph@gmail.com**. Please include what happened, where it happened, and any information needed to follow up. Maintainers will review reports and handle them as confidentially as reasonably possible.
